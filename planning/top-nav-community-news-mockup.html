About

I am a Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at Pundra University of Science & Technology. I am interested in what AI models learn and how we can examine their behaviour. My current interests include AI safety and interpretability, computer vision, large language models, and vision-language models. I am looking for PhD opportunities beginning in Fall 2028.
